{\footnotesize
\noindent\textit{Notes:} This table compares league-wide predicted Black shares with the shares published by The Institute for Diversity and Ethics in Sport (TIDES), \textit{Racial and Gender Report Card: National Football League}. Panel A reports shares in percent by season. Pred.\ is the mean primary predicted probability of being non-Hispanic Black alone (model-only; documented race is not used); Pred.\ B+m adds the predicted probability of two or more races, which includes non-Black multiracial persons and so bounds Black alone or in combination from above; PredDoc is the documented-race sensitivity variant; BIFSG is the names-and-county posterior with Census population priors and no NFL prior. Players are game-day roster members (Active and Inactive rows of the regular-season weekly roster in weeks in which the team played), weighted by game-day weeks. Head coaches are non-interim head coaches, one per franchise. Assistant coaches are all coaches below the head coach (coordinators, position, assistant and quality-control, and strength-and-conditioning coaches; support-only staff excluded), one row per person, franchise and season (423--880 a season; TIDES prints totals of 537 in 2012 and 631 in 2018). TIDES definitions: through 2016 the player share is `African-American', coded by TIDES from team media guides, with each player in one category (multiracial players inside a single race), so it lies between Black alone and Black alone or in combination; from 2019 it is self-identified `Black or African American', which excludes players who report two or more races (9--11\%) or do not disclose (3--9\%); the drop between 2016 and 2019 is largely this definitional break. TIDES +2+ adds two or more races. Diff.\ is Pred.\ minus TIDES; from 2019 the TIDES Black share has non-disclosers in its denominator, so Diff.\ rises with non-disclosure. Diff.\ B+m is Pred.\ B+m minus TIDES +2+ where TIDES reports two or more races, else minus the `African-American' share. Each season uses the report's own contemporaneous figure (the season the report states); $^{r}$ marks values taken from the retrospective appendix of the 2023 report because no contemporaneous percentage exists; the staff two-or-more and not-disclosed shares (assistant coaches 2018--2023, head coaches 2021--2023) all come from that appendix. -- marks seasons without a TIDES figure (players: 2002, 2004, 2017--2018, 2024--2025; no report covers 2024 or 2025). Panel B compares each model series with TIDES over the seasons of the period that have the TIDES figure: mean difference and mean absolute difference in percentage points, and the correlation across seasons. Pred.\ B+m is not compared with Black alone. Cells that average fewer seasons than the row states: players against the TIDES share: 2003, 2005--2016; assistant coaches against the TIDES share: 2002--2003, 2005--2016; head coaches against Black + two or more: 2021--2023; head coaches against the disclosure-adjusted share: 2023. TIDES conflicts kept as printed: the 2002 assistant-coach figure may refer to 2001; the 2004 head-coach share (16\%, before the season) differs from the three Black head coaches reported for 2004 in the next report. The weekly rosters list 1,687--1,692 distinct players a season through 2015, 2,648 in 2016 and 2,110--2,373 from 2017; with each player-season weighted once (headcount), the player gap of Pred.\ is -9.0 pp in 2010--2015 against `African-American' and -7.6 pp in 2019--2023 against the disclosure-adjusted share. For players, most of the shortfall of Pred.\ against `African-American' (-9.8 pp through 2016) is the multiracial mass: the mean predicted probability of two or more races on game-day rosters is 8.0\%, Pred.\ B+m differs from `African-American' by +0.2 pp in 2010--2015 and from Black + two or more by +6.5 pp in 2019--2023, and the model separates multiracial from Black alone only weakly. For coaches the level shortfall survives: Pred.\ B+m is -9.9 pp from the `African-American' assistant-coach share through 2016, consistent with name likelihoods that understate Black names among coaches. Head-coach season errors are large and offsetting: through 2016 the mean absolute difference of Pred.\ is 3.3 pp against a mean difference of -1.3 pp, and its correlation with TIDES across seasons is 0.68 (-- marks a correlation that is undefined because the TIDES share is constant). For head coaches PredDoc is close to documented race: documentation is positive-only and concentrated among Black head coaches (19 of the 28 documented among 108 head coaches of 2010--2025 are Black), and PredDoc sets documented persons to their documented race, so its fit to TIDES checks documentation, not the model. Race is predicted, not observed: each person's probability combines first name, surname and hometown county (BIFSG) with an NFL prior estimated by EM on predetermined characteristics (players: entry position, rookie era, draft round, college type, county availability; staff: first role group, unit and era); documented race is not used (notes/race-prediction-design.md). A league share is the mean member probability; it is unbiased for the true share when the probabilities are calibrated, so level gaps against TIDES measure miscalibration.
